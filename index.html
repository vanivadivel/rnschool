Oxford Kid Lifecycle

A Day in the Life of a Kid at Oxford

We structure the child's daily schedule specifically to keep them excited, cognitive-active, and physically healthy. Play periods alternate with modern learning segments to ensure overall growth.

09:00 AM - 09:30 AM

Circle Time & Morning Meet-up

Cognitive Phonics & Alphabets

11:30 AM - 12:30 PM

Creative Playground & Activity
